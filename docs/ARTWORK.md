# Artwork provenance

The collection-satin.webp, collection-embossed.webp and collection-garden.webp files were generated specifically for VELORA with the built-in image generation tool and converted to WebP.

Prompt set:
1. Original portrait ivory folded wedding stationery, realistic satin bow at lower third, translucent vellum and jasmine, blank upper paper for overlaid text, no text or watermark.
2. Original portrait deep teal envelope with dimensional antique gold and blue acanthus embossing, lower gold seal, empty upper centre, no mandala/text/watermark.
3. Original portrait Sri Lankan jasmine and rose garden arch at sunset, ivory sari and suit couple at bottom, quiet cream arch opening for typography, painterly realism, no text/watermark.

Other heritage, skyline, coastal road and lace assets generated earlier for VELORA are retained. The four kandyan-ride images were extracted from the HTML supplied by the user. Other images were present in the original user repository. Confirm rights to supplied/retained assets before commercial use. No Eventoura or Invitation.lk artwork, video, audio or source files were imported. New SVG/CSS decorative elements are editable in CelebrationExperience.tsx and celebrations.css.


## v3.1 original reference-study artwork
Generated with the built-in image tool and converted into the project:
- `public/images/reference-royal-cover.webp`: blue velvet envelope, sculpted ivory/blue acanthus, gold edges and luminous heart seal; no text.
- `public/images/reference-garden-cover.webp`: original full-length Sri Lankan Nilame/osariya portrait under jasmine and roses, warm stone arch and oil lamps; no text.
- `public/images/reference-royal-paper.webp`: blank ivory handmade paper with narrow blue/ivory floral scrollwork and gold edges.
- `public/images/reference-garden-paper.webp`: empty warm gold parchment centre, bells and jasmine at the top, oil lamps and lotus in the lower corners.
Prompt set requested original portrait compositions, quiet typography areas, realistic embossed/painterly materials, no UI, logo or watermark. Cinzel Decorative and Great Vibes are locally bundled with their font licences.


## v4 original pearl artwork and films
Built-in image generation produced couture-pearl-cover.webp (raised ivory Beeralu papercut floral envelope, pearlescent floral seal, blank top area) and couture-pearl-paper.webp (empty ivory cotton paper with narrow lace and pearl edge strips). Prompts specified realistic dimensional white-on-ivory materials, no text/UI/logo/watermark. These were converted to WebP and used with retained original project assets to composite three local seven-second MP4 films with FFmpeg/Pillow: moving envelope halves and rising letter, pearl-to-gold seal tint and lace opening, rotating lotus geometry/couple reveal/particles. No reference video was downloaded or reproduced frame-for-frame.
